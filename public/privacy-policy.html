Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026  |  Lakshya Education / Bhoomi Connect CRM

1. Introduction

Lakshya Education ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Bhoomi Connect CRM platform and associated services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you interact with our services, including communication via WhatsApp Business API.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification: Name, phone number, email address
  • Academic Information: Courses of interest, academic year, school/college name
  • Communication Data: WhatsApp messages sent/received through our business number
  • Enquiry Data: Counseling notes, pipeline stage, fee discussions

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To contact prospective students about NEET/JEE coaching programs
  • To send academic updates, admission information, and counseling reminders
  • To manage student enquiries through our internal CRM system
  • To send WhatsApp messages via Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API
  • To improve our admission counseling and support processes

4. WhatsApp Messaging

We use Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API to communicate with prospective students. By providing your phone number and consenting to contact, you agree to receive WhatsApp messages from Lakshya Education. You may opt out at any time by replying "STOP" to any message.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared with:

  • Internal staff (counselors, faculty) on a need-to-know basis
  • Meta Platforms (for WhatsApp Business API services)
  • Firebase (Google) for secure cloud data storage

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) with industry-standard encryption.

7. Your Rights

  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
